# Contributing

Suggestions are welcome. Open a GitHub issue for each add or removal.

## Suggest a domain

Include:

- The hostname (no `www.`, no path).
- Why its primary product is generative AI or an LLM (a link to the product page helps).
- Which list it belongs on: core ([lists/domains.txt](lists/domains.txt)) or extended ([lists/domains-extended.txt](lists/domains-extended.txt)).

## Suggest a removal

Include:

- The hostname.
- Why it should come off (false positive, the site only uses AI as a feature, it breaks something important such as model downloads, or the domain is dead or parked).

YouTube, general search engines, social networks, and other sites that only ship AI as a feature stay off the list on purpose. Path-based AI buttons on those sites belong in a cosmetic list such as [Stevo's AI Blocklist](https://github.com/Stevoisiak/Stevos-AI-Blocklist), since DNS cannot block a URL path.

Use the issue templates when they are available: "Add a domain" and "Remove a domain".
